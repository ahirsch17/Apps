# GenZ College Student Usability Audit

## TL;DR: What Would Actually Stop Me From Using This

### 🚩 IMMEDIATE RED FLAGS

#### 1. **"What are you up for?" - Cringe Alert**
```
Current:
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ What are you up for?            │ ← Sounds like my mom asking
│ [🍔 Hungry] [📚 Study]          │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Why this sucks**:
- No one talks like this in 2026
- Sounds corporate/boomer
- Makes me feel like I'm filling out a form

**What we actually say**:
- "down for food?"
- "tryna study?"
- "gym rn?"

**Fix**: "Down for..." or "Looking for..." or just show icons with no header

---

#### 2. **"I'm free right now" Button - Anxiety Trigger**

```
Current:
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ ✨ I'm free right now           │ ← TOO COMMITTAL
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Why this is terrifying**:
- What if someone messages me and I don't want to hang?
- What if I say I'm free but then want to nap?
- What if no one responds? (rejection anxiety)
- What if TOO many people respond? (social exhaustion)

**Real student behavior**:
- We avoid commitment until last second
- We want plausible deniability
- We ghost plans constantly
- We prefer passive "maybe available" signals

**Fix**: 
- "Available later" (not "now")
- "Down to grab food" (specific, low stakes)
- Time-limited status (auto-expires in 30 min)
- Easy way to cancel without looking flaky

---

#### 3. **Activity Modes - Embarrassing**

```
Current:
[🍔 Hungry] [📚 Study] [🏃 Gym] [😊 Social] [🌙 Quiet]
```

**Why I would NEVER use this**:
- **"Hungry mode"** - Do I want everyone knowing I'm hungry? Feels vulnerable
- **"Study mode"** - Everyone will judge me for studying on a Friday
- **"Gym mode"** - Feels like humble-bragging
- **"Social mode"** - Desperate vibes
- **"Quiet mode"** - Why am I on a social app then?

**Real student behavior**:
- We share vibes through stories (music, food pics, locations)
- We use Snap Maps passively
- We say "studying at Starbucks if anyone wants to join" (open invite, no pressure)
- We don't broadcast our "mode" - that's weird

**Fix**:
- Replace with location-based "at X doing Y" status
- Make it story-like (ephemeral, expires)
- Allow friends to "join" or "wave" with low commitment
- Copy BeReal's "your friends are at Squires" vibe

---

#### 4. **Spontaneous Plan Suggestions - Too Pushy**

```
Current:
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ 💡 Suggested                    │
│ Coffee with John in 15 min?     │ ← Feels obligated
│ [Yes, let's do it!] [Not now]   │ ← Too formal
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Why this makes me anxious**:
- 15 minutes? I need AT LEAST 30 min to decide
- "Yes, let's do it!" is too enthusiastic
- What if I click "Not now" and John sees?
- The app is making decisions FOR me (not with me)

**Real student behavior**:
- We text "wya" and see what happens
- We make plans 10 minutes before, not 15
- We prefer group hangs (safety in numbers)
- We want receipts read = low pressure

**Fix**:
- Show "John is at Squires" (not a suggestion)
- Let ME decide to message them
- Group suggestions: "3 friends at Newman" > "Coffee with John"
- Make it passive, not active

---

#### 5. **Too Many Cards = Information Overload**

```
Current Today Screen:
├── Greeting
├── Headline card
├── Friends free NOW (5 large cards)    ← FIVE CARDS?
├── Next class card
├── Meetups (3 cards)                   ← THREE MORE?
├── Quick Actions card
├── Events card
├── Spontaneous Plans card
└── Schedule section

Total: ~13 cards to process
```

**Why this is overwhelming**:
- I have 3 seconds to check this app between classes
- I just want to know: "anyone free?" or "what's happening tonight?"
- Everything looks equally important (no hierarchy)
- I have to scroll and read too much
- Decision paralysis - too many options

**Real student behavior**:
- Open app for 2-5 seconds MAX
- Scan for familiar faces
- Close app
- Maybe message one person

**What we ACTUALLY want to see in 3 seconds**:
1. **Who's free right now?** (3 friends max, not 5)
2. **What's tonight?** (1 event, not a list)
3. **Unread messages** (if any)

That's it. Everything else is noise.

---

#### 6. **Distance Labels - Creepy Vibes**

```
Current:
John 🍔
📍 Squires • 4 min ← How does the app know this?
[💬 Message]
```

**Why this feels invasive**:
- I didn't consent to sharing my exact location
- "4 min" implies GPS tracking
- What if I'm at my ex's dorm? 
- What if I skip class and don't want people knowing?
- Feels like I'm being watched

**Privacy concerns**:
- GenZ is privacy-conscious (grew up with data breaches)
- We want control over who sees what
- Snap Maps has ghost mode for a reason
- We share locations voluntarily (dropping pin), not constantly

**Fix**:
- Only show building name, not distance
- Let users opt-in to precise location
- Make it ephemeral (expires after 1 hour)
- Clear privacy controls upfront

---

#### 7. **Message Buttons Everywhere - Choice Overload**

```
Current: Every friend has [💬 Message] button
```

**Why this is exhausting**:
- I don't want to message 5 different people
- What do I even say? "Hey you're free"? Awkward
- What if they don't respond? Rejection
- What if I have to have 5 conversations?

**Real student behavior**:
- We already have group chats
- We use Snap/iMessage for 1:1
- We send memes, not "hey wanna hang?"
- We prefer passive engagement (likes, reactions)

**Fix**:
- Remove individual message buttons
- Show "3 friends free - start a group chat?"
- Or integrate with existing messaging (deeplink to iMessage)
- Or "wave" button (low commitment acknowledgment)

### 🎯 WHAT WOULD ACTUALLY MAKE ME USE THIS APP

## 🧠 UNDERSTANDING ACTUAL STUDENT SCHEDULES & HABITS

### Monday-Thursday Reality:
- **8am-2pm**: Classes (can't hang)
- **2pm-5pm**: "Library" (actually scrolling TikTok)
- **5pm-7pm**: Dinner window (prime social time)
- **7pm-10pm**: Clubs/gym/homework
- **10pm-2am**: Social time (spontaneous hangouts, late food runs)

### Friday Reality:
- **Morning**: Minimal classes (most skip)
- **Afternoon**: Pre-game naps
- **Evening**: Going out

### Weekend Reality:
- **Saturday**: Recover from Friday
- **Sunday**: Sunday scaries (anxiety, homework panic)

### Real Pain Points:
1. **Eating alone** - most awkward/lonely moment
2. **Studying alone** - procrastination trap
3. **Missing out** - FOMO when everyone posts stories
4. **New city** - don't know anyone yet (freshmen)
5. **Social exhaustion** - want to be invited but also want to cancel

### What We DON'T Care About:
- Knowing our schedule (we memorized it week 2)
- Planning hangouts days in advance
- Formal "meetup windows"
- Complex privacy settings
- Reading instructions

## 🚨 SPECIFIC CURRENT DESIGN ISSUES

### Issue 1: Headline Card Copy

```
Current: "You've got time with John 'til 3:00pm"
```

**Problems**:
- "You've got time with" - weird phrasing
- Assumes I want to hang with John (maybe I don't?)
- Time commitment feels long

**Better**: 
- "John's free til 3pm" (factual, not prescriptive)
- Or just show this in the friend card, no headline needed

---

### Issue 2: "Find Time Together" Section

```
Current:
Find time together
Shared free windows today

🍽️ Lunch: 12:30 - 1:30pm
With John • Good for 1 hour
```

**Problems**:
- "Find time together" - sounds like a work meeting
- "Shared free windows" - corporate jargon
- "Good for 1 hour" - I'll decide how long, thanks
- Too planning-focused (we don't plan like this)

**Reality**:
- I text John at 12:25: "food?"
- He says "ye"
- We meet at 12:35
- No "shared free windows" needed

**Better**: Remove this entirely OR change to:
- "Free til 2pm - so is John" (simple status)

---

### Issue 3: Next Class Card

```
Current:
Next class
CALC 2
2:00 - 3:15pm
McBryde 100
[Classmates button]
```

**What's good**:
- Shows time (actually useful)
- Classmates button (good for "who's in my section?")

**What's weird**:
- I already know my next class
- This info is in my calendar already
- Takes up space

**Better**:
- Make it smaller (not a full card)
- Or remove entirely
- Or only show if class starts in <15 min

---

### Issue 4: Friends Free Now Cards

```
Current:
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🅰️ John 🍔                      │
│ 📍 Squires • 4 min              │
│                   [💬 Message]  │
└─────────────────────────────────┘

Repeated for FIVE friends

**Problems**:
- Too many cards (3 max)

└─────────────────────────────────┘

One card, multiple people, tap faces to see more
```

---

### Issue 5: Events

```
Current:
🏐 IM Volleyball • Tonight 6pm
[John][Rachel][+6 interested]
3 looking for partners
```

**What's good**:
- Tonight's event (relevant)
- Shows who's going (social proof)

## 📱 WHAT OTHER APPS DO BETTER

### BeReal:
- **2-min window** - creates urgency, gets quick engagement
- **Friends first** - see your friends, not strangers
- **No likes/comments** - low pressure
- **Simple**: Take photo → See friends' photos → Done

**What Between could learn**: Urgency + Simplicity

---

### Snap Maps:
- **Passive location sharing** - I can see where friends are WITHOUT asking
- **Ghost mode** - easy privacy control
- **Bitmoji on map** - playful, not creepy
- **Stories** - see what friends are doing

**What Between could learn**: Passive discovery, visual map

---

### iMessage:
- **Fast** - message appears instantly
- **Read receipts** - low pressure communication
- **Reactions** - can acknowledge without replying
- **Group chats** - spontaneous plans happen here

**What Between could learn**: Speed, reactions, groups

---

### Discord:
- **Channels** - organized by topic/purpose
- **Voice channels** - see who's in voice (passive hanging out)
- **Status** - "playing X" "in a call" etc
- **Roles** - identity without forms

**What Between could learn**: Passive presence, status

## 🚀 ONE BOLD IDEA

```
Open app → See map of campus → Friends are dots → Tap to see details
```

**Why this works**:
- Visual > Text (scan in 2 seconds)
- Context: "Oh John's at Squires, I'm at D2, let's meet halfway"
- Familiar: Everyone knows Snap Maps
- Scalable: Works with 3 friends or 30
- Privacy: Can ghost mode
- Fun: Feels like a game

- Battery drain
- Privacy concerns
- Snap Maps already exists

## BOTTOM LINE

**The app is trying to PLAN social interactions.**
**GenZ doesn't plan. We vibe.**

Make it:
- **Faster** (3-second rule)
- **Simpler** (3 cards max)
- **Passive** (show, don't suggest)
- **Authentic** (real language)
- **Private** (opt-in everything)

And maybe, MAYBE students will actually use it.
