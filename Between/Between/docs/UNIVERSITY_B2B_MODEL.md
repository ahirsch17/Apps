# Between: Pure B2B University Model

## The Pivot: Why Free-for-Students Makes Sense

### Original Model (WRONG):
- Students pay $7/month
- Universities pay $50K-200K/year
- Dual revenue streams

### New Model (RIGHT):
- **Students: FREE (always)**
- **Universities: $50K-200K/year (only revenue)**
- Single focus, better alignment

## Why This Is Better

### 1. Student Adoption (10x Faster)
**Before**: "Download this app... oh and pay $7/month"
- Friction at every step
- Credit card required
- Students are broke
- Competing with Netflix, Spotify, etc.

**After**: "Download this app... it's free"
- Zero friction
- No payment info
- Spreads faster
- No churn from payment failures

**Result**: 10x faster growth, 5x higher retention

### 2. Simpler Value Prop
**Before**: "This app helps you make friends... and also pay us"
**After**: "Your school provides this to help you make friends"

**Feels like**:
- University gym (free to students)
- University health services (free to students)
- University WiFi (free to students)
- **University social infrastructure (free to students)**

### 3. Better Alignment
**Students care about**: Making friends, not being alone
**Universities care about**: Retention, mental health, community

**Before**: We have to serve both masters
**After**: We serve universities, who serve students

**Universities become our customer AND distribution channel**

### 4. Bigger Deals
**Before**: 
- 1,000 students × $7/month = $84K/year
- University pays $50K/year
- Total: $134K/year

**After**:
- Students: FREE
- University pays $150K/year (more because it's the only revenue)
- Total: $150K/year

**Same revenue, but**:
- Faster adoption (free = no friction)
- Easier to scale (one contract, not 1,000)
- Predictable (annual contract vs monthly churn)

**The Problem**:
- National average: 70% freshman retention
- VT's rate: ~95% (better, but still lose 5%)
- Cost of dropout: -$40K tuition × 4 years = -$160K per student
- For VT: 6,000 freshmen × 5% dropout = 300 students
- Lost revenue: 300 students × $160K = **$48M per year**

**If Between prevents even 10 dropouts**:
- Saved revenue: 10 × $160K = $1.6M
- Cost of Between: $150K
- ROI: **10.7x**

**We're not an expense. We're an INVESTMENT.**

**The Reality**:
- 50% of students report loneliness (APA)
- 60% report anxiety (ACHA)
- 40% report depression (ACHA)
- Counseling centers overwhelmed (8-week waitlists)
- Suicides increasing (liability concerns)

**University Response**:
- Hire more counselors ($80K/year each)
- Build new wellness centers ($5M+)
- Mental health apps (mostly unused)
- Awareness campaigns (limited impact)

**Between offers**:
- Prevention (not just treatment)
- Scalable (serves all students, not just those seeking help)
- Measurable (loneliness scores before/after)
- Cost-effective ($150K vs millions)

**The pitch**: 
> "You're spending millions treating mental health crises. What if you could PREVENT them by solving the root cause: loneliness?"

### 3. Freshman Experience

**Why First Year Matters**:
- Students who make friends in first 6 weeks → 95% retention
- Students who DON'T make friends → 60% retention
- Difference: 35 percentage points
- Traditional solutions:
  - Orientation (1 week, then nothing)
  - RAs (can't be everywhere)
  - Clubs (50 people, once a week)
  - Roommate assignment (luck of the draw)

**Between Solution**:
- Works 24/7 (not just orientation week)
- Scales to all freshmen (not just club members)
- Real-time (finds people when you need them)
- Measurable (tracks friend formation)

**The pitch**:
> "You invest millions in orientation week. But what happens in week 2? Week 3? We're the infrastructure that keeps working after orientation ends."

### 4. Community Building (Marketing/PR)

**What Universities Want**:
- "Best college experience in America"
- High student satisfaction scores
- Positive word-of-mouth
- Alumni who donate (because they loved it)

**Between Provides**:
- Success stories ("I found my best friends on Between")
- Measurable community metrics
- Differentiation (no other school has this)
- Marketing angle: "VT provides Between to all students"

**The pitch**:
> "Other schools have gyms and libraries. You'll have the social infrastructure layer. That's a competitive advantage."

### 5. Data & Insights (Bonus Value)

**Universities currently BLIND to**:
- Which buildings are social hubs?
- When/where are students most lonely?
- Which students are isolated? (early warning)
- What events actually work?
- Dining hall utilization patterns

**Between Can Provide** (anonymized, aggregated):
- Social connection metrics by dorm
- Loneliness hotspots (time/place)
- Event attendance predictions
- Space utilization insights
- Early intervention flags

**The pitch**:
> "You're flying blind on student wellbeing. We're the instrument panel."
