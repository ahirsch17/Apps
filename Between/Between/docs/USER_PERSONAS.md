# User Personas: Between App

## Persona 1: Emma the Freshman 🎒

### Demographics
- **Age**: 18
- **Year**: Freshman
- **Major**: Undecided (thinking Biology)
- **Living**: Pritchard Hall (on-campus dorm)
- **Roommate**: Random assignment, nice but different interests
- **Home**: 4 hours away, first time away from home

### Daily Reality

**Physical Environment**:
- Surrounded by people 24/7
- Dining hall = awkward if alone
- Walks to class past hundreds of students
- Hears neighbors having fun through walls
- Forced to attend floor meetings, RAs, events

**Social Situation**:
- Knows 100+ faces, 5 names
- Has roommate's friends (don't vibe)
- Group chat from orientation (dead after week 2)
- Sits alone in dining hall scrolling phone
- Sees Snaps of everyone having fun

**Academic**:
- CHEM 1035 lecture hall: 300 students
- CALC 1225: 40 students, sits in back alone
- Knows NO ONE in her classes
- Too anxious to ask "is anyone free after?"
- Studies alone in Newman Library

### Current Pain Points

**The Proximity Paradox**:
> "I'm surrounded by thousands of people but feel completely alone."

**Specific Struggles**:
1. **Meal Anxiety** - "Do I eat alone AGAIN or skip lunch?"
2. **Weekend FOMO** - Sees everyone's stories, not invited
3. **Study Isolation** - Procrastinates alone, no accountability
4. **Class Loneliness** - Sits near same people, never talks
5. **Roommate Mismatch** - Nice girl, but goes to bed at 9pm
6. **Friend Group Gatekeeping** - Everyone already has their people
7. **Overthinking** - "If I ask, will they think I'm desperate?"

**The Invisible Wall**:
- 🚫 Can't just walk up to strangers
- 🚫 Too awkward to ask "wanna grab food?"
- 🚫 Doesn't know who else is alone
- 🚫 Misses signals (that girl in CALC also eats alone!)
- 🚫 No excuse to reach out ("hey random person...")

### Current Behavior (Without Between)

**Monday-Friday**:
- 8am: Wake up to roommate's alarm (already gone to 8am class)
- 9am: CHEM lecture, sits in same seat, talks to no one
- 11am: Grabs food alone, eats in dorm scrolling TikTok
- 1pm: CALC, sees same girl every day, never says hi
- 3pm: Library, studies alone, procrastinates on phone
- 6pm: Dining hall alone or skips dinner (anxiety)
- 8pm: Dorm room, hears neighbors laughing
- 10pm: Scrolls Instagram, everyone posting group pics
- 11pm: Texts high school friends (they're busy)
- 12am: Feels lonely, falls asleep

**Weekend**:
- Sees Snaps of parties, not invited
- Stays in dorm, calls mom
- "How's college?" "Good!" (lying)

**What She Does Instead**:
- Joins 5 clubs (goes to 2 meetings, then stops)
- Swipes through Tinder (not really looking)
- Posts thirst traps hoping for engagement
- Pretends to be busy when actually alone

### How Between Solves This

**Core Problem**: Emma doesn't know WHO ELSE is alone/available.

**Between Shows Her**:
```
Open app at 11:05am after CHEM:

Free right now (3)
├── Sarah - Newman Library
├── Mike - Squires 
└── Jenna - D2

Next class
CALC 1225 - 1:00pm

Free later today
12:30 - 1:00pm
Sarah, Mike
```

**The Moment**:
> "Wait, Sarah from my CALC class is at Newman? And she's free? I could grab lunch..."

### Emma's User Journey

**Week 1** (Discovery):
- Downloads Between (RA recommended)
- Sees 5 people from her floor
- Sees 3 people from CHEM
- "Oh wow, Mike lives in my building?"

**Week 2** (First Action):
- 11:30am, hungry, opens app
- Sees Sarah free at D2
- Texts her: "yo are you at d2? im starving"
- Sarah: "YESS come thru"
- **Eats lunch with someone for the first time**

**Week 3** (Habit Forms):
- Checks Between before meals
- Sees recurring overlaps with same people
- "Sarah and I are free at the same time every Tues/Thurs"
- Starts going to library with Mike (CHEM study buddy)

**Week 4** (Friend Group Emerges):
- Sarah introduces Emma to her friends
- Now in group chat
- Gets invited to weekend plans
- **No longer alone**

### Key Features for Emma

**Must Have**:
1. ✅ **Class integration** - "Who's in my CHEM section?"
2. ✅ **Meal times** - "Who's free for lunch RIGHT NOW?"
3. ✅ **Study buddies** - "Who else is studying?"
4. ✅ **Location-based** - "Who's in my building?"
5. ✅ **Low commitment** - Don't need to commit to plans

**Nice to Have**:
- Icebreaker prompts (what to say?)
- Group formation (3+ people easier than 1:1)
- Event discovery (Freshman Council events)
- Success stories ("Sarah met her best friend on Between!")

### Emma's Quote
> "I was eating alone every day until I realized half my floor was also eating alone at the same time. Now we have a lunch group. I just needed to know WHO and WHEN."

## Persona 2: Jake the Junior 🏠

### Demographics
- **Age**: 20
- **Year**: Junior
- **Major**: Computer Science
- **Living**: Off-campus apartment (0.8 miles away)
- **Roommates**: 2 CS majors (barely see them)
- **Home**: In-state, goes home some weekends

### Daily Reality

**Physical Environment**:
- Private bedroom (door closed)
- Kitchen (sees roommates for 5 min)
- Walks to campus = 15 min (skips if online option)
- Campus = sea of strangers now
- No forced social interactions

**Social Situation**:
- Has friend group from freshman year
- But everyone's busy now
- Haven't hung out in weeks
- Group chat mostly memes, no plans
- Saw Mike at Squires, didn't say hi (headphones on)

**Academic**:
- CS 3114: Half empty, everyone remote
- MATH elective: Goes 50% of the time
- Didn't know Emma from HS was in his MATH class (she sits far away)
- Studies at home (easier than campus)
- Group projects = awkward Zoom calls

### Current Pain Points

**The Isolation Spiral**:
> "It's just... easier to stay home."

**How It Starts**:
1. **Week 1**: "I'll go to class and catch up with people"
2. **Week 3**: "This lecture is recorded, I'll watch later" (never does)
3. **Week 5**: "I haven't seen anyone in 2 weeks"
4. **Week 8**: "I don't even know who's in my classes"
5. **Week 12**: "Am I depressed or just lazy?"

**Specific Struggles**:
1. **No Forcing Function** - Nothing makes him leave his room
2. **Passive Drift** - Friendships fade from neglect
3. **Discovery Problem** - Doesn't know who's around
4. **High Friction** - "I'd have to text, coordinate, commute..."
5. **Analysis Paralysis** - "Are they even on campus today?"
6. **Social Atrophy** - Out of practice, feels awkward
7. **False Busyness** - "I have so much work" (scrolling Reddit)

**The Missed Connections**:
- 🚫 Friend Mike was at Starbucks, Jake was at Newman (both alone)
- 🚫 Group chat: "anyone free?" Jake sees it 2 hours later
- 🚫 Classmate Emma walks past him daily (he has AirPods in)
- 🚫 Gym at 6pm, roommate went at 6:30pm (didn't coordinate)
- 🚫 Skipped IM volleyball, didn't know friends were going

### Current Behavior (Without Between)

**Monday-Friday**:
- 10am: Wake up, roommate already left
- 11am: CS 3114 recorded lecture (watches at 1.5x speed)
- 12pm: Kitchen, microwaves leftovers, back to room
- 1pm: Reddit, Twitter, YouTube (procrastinating)
- 3pm: "I should go to campus..." (doesn't)
- 5pm: Starts homework (due tonight)
- 7pm: Roommate comes home, "Yo" "Sup" (back to rooms)
- 9pm: Finish homework, game until 2am
- **Never left his room**

**Weekend**:
- Sleeps til noon
- Scrolls TikTok for 3 hours
- "I should reach out to people..." (doesn't)
- Orders DoorDash
- Plays League

**Rare Social Interaction**:
- Group chat: "anyone tryna get food?"
- Jake: "yeah im down" (15 min later)
- Friend: "nvm we already went"
- Jake: "oh aight" (back to room)

### How Between Solves This

**Core Problem**: Jake doesn't know his friends are ALREADY on campus/nearby.

**Between Shows Him**:
```
Open app at 12:30pm:

Free right now (4)
├── Mike - Starbucks (study mode)
├── Sarah - Squires (grabbed lunch)
├── Alex - Rec Center (gym)
└── Emma - Newman Library

IM Volleyball - Tonight 6pm
5 friends going • 2 need partners
```

**The Moment**:
> "Wait, Mike is at Starbucks right now? I was about to go there anyway..."

### Jake's User Journey

**Week 1** (Skeptical):
- Friend sends Between link
- "Another app? Whatever"
- Installs, doesn't open for 3 days

**Week 2** (First Surprise):
- Opens app randomly at 1pm
- Sees Mike at Tompkins
- "Yo I didn't know you were on campus"
- Mike: "bruh I'm here every day"
- Jake: "..." (realizes how isolated he's become)

**Week 3** (Convenience Hook):
- About to walk to Starbucks
- Checks Between
- Mike already there
- "Spontaneous study session" (didn't have to plan)

**Week 4** (Rediscovery):
- Sees Emma in Between
- "Wait, Emma from HS? She's at VT?"
- Messages her: "yooo I had no idea you went here"
- Turns out she's in his MATH class
- They've been in the same room for 8 weeks

**Week 8** (Habit Change):
- Now checks Between before leaving apartment
- "Anyone already on campus?"
- Coordinates gym time with roommate
- Goes to IM volleyball (saw friends were going)
- **Leaves apartment 5x/week instead of 2x**

### Key Features for Jake

**Must Have**:
1. ✅ **Real-time location** - "Who's already on campus?"
2. ✅ **Low friction** - No planning, just "I'm here too"
3. ✅ **Friend discovery** - "Wait, they go here?"
4. ✅ **Event coordination** - "Who's going to X?"
5. ✅ **Passive presence** - See without asking

**Nice to Have**:
- "Commute together?" (both going to campus)
- "Last minute plans" (whoever's free in 10 min)
- Study spot crowding (is Tompkins full?)
- Friend streaks (reconnect with faded friends)

### Jake's Quote
> "I was spending 23 hours a day alone in my apartment. I didn't even know my friends were on campus. Now I actually see people. Not because I 'planned' anything—I just know who's around."

## Shared Needs (Both Personas)

### Universal Pain Points:
1. **Don't know who's available** (invisible to each other)
2. **Too much friction** (texting, coordinating, waiting)
3. **Fear of rejection** (what if they say no?)
4. **Missed connections** (pass each other daily, never talk)
5. **No excuse to reach out** (need a reason)

### What Both Want:
- 🎯 **Spontaneity** - Not planning days ahead
- 🎯 **Low commitment** - Can say no without guilt
- 🎯 **Passive discovery** - See without asking
- 🎯 **Real-time** - Who's free RIGHT NOW?
- 🎯 **Context** - Why should I message them? (they're at Starbucks too)

### How Between Helps Both:
1. **Visibility** - See who's available/nearby
2. **Context** - Reason to reach out ("I'm heading to D2 too")
3. **Low friction** - No planning, just show up
4. **Non-awkward** - App provides the excuse
5. **Reciprocal** - Both people benefit from interaction

## Key Insights for Product

### Design for Two Modes:

**Emma Mode (Discovery)**:
- Show WHO (faces, names, classes)
- Emphasize "others like you" (alone too)
- Group formation (3+ easier than 1:1)
- Icebreakers and prompts

**Jake Mode (Coordination)**:
- Show WHERE (locations, real-time)
- Emphasize "already there" (no planning needed)
- Quick actions ("join them")
- Passive presence (don't need to message)

### Feature Priority:

**For Emma**:
1. Class integration (find study buddies)
2. Meal times (solve dining hall anxiety)
3. Dorm proximity (who's in my building?)
4. Events (meet new people)

**For Jake**:
5. Real-time location (who's on campus?)
6. Friend status (are they even in town?)
7. Last-minute plans (spontaneous coordination)
8. Group activities (join existing plans)

### Success Metrics:

**Emma**:
- Time to first friend made
- Meals eaten alone → with others
- Number of new connections
- Weekly active rate

**Jake**:
- Days out of apartment per week
- Reconnections with old friends
- Spontaneous meetups vs planned
- Retention rate

## Pitch Deck Angle

### The Problem (Two Sides):

**Freshman Paradox**:
> "18,000 students on campus. I know 3 people."

**Upperclassman Crisis**:
> "My best friend was at Starbucks. I was at Newman. Both of us alone. Neither of us knew."

### The Insight:

**Loneliness isn't about being alone. It's about being INVISIBLE to the people who'd want to hang out.**

- Emma is eating lunch alone
- Sarah is ALSO eating lunch alone
- **They don't know about each other**

### The Solution:

Between makes you **visible** to the people who matter, at the moment it matters.

### The Impact:

**For Emma**: Finds her people in the first 2 weeks (not first 2 months)

**For Jake**: Stays connected instead of drifting into isolation

### The Market:

- **5.2M freshmen** start college every year (all Emmas)
- **50%** report feeling lonely (source: APA)
- **60%** of upperclassmen live off-campus (all potential Jakes)
- **Mental health crisis** on campuses nationwide

### Why Now:

- Post-COVID social atrophy
- Mental health awareness
- Gen Z craves IRL connection
- Existing tools (group chats, Snap Maps) don't solve this

### The Vision:

**"Between" = The layer between your close friends and strangers. The people you'd hang out with if you just knew they were available.**
