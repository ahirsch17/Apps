## VT-Specific ROI Calculation

### Current State:

**Freshmen who leave** (~325 per year):
- 325 freshmen × $35K/year in-state tuition = $11.4M/year loss
- 325 freshmen × 4 years = $45.6M lifetime loss

**Out-of-state students** (60% of freshmen):
- Average tuition: $55K/year
- 195 out-of-state freshmen leave
- 195 × $55K × 4 years = $42.9M

**Total retention loss**: ~$50M per year in lifetime tuition

### With Between:

**If Between prevents just 15 freshmen from leaving**:
- 15 students × $180K (avg 4-year cost) = $2.7M saved
- Cost of Between: $150K/year
- **ROI: 18x**

**If Between prevents 30 freshmen** (10% of dropouts):
- 30 students × $180K = $5.4M saved
- **ROI: 36x**

**Even conservative estimates (10 students)**: 12x ROI
