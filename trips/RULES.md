# Trip planning rules

Hard filters. If a pick fails one, drop it or mark it optional with the reason. Alex can overrule a rule in chat; until then, follow it.

## Keep the page true

- **Teralinks:** every flight row’s Capital One URL must match that exact route, date, and passenger count. If the itinerary changes, rewrite the link in the same edit.
- **Not purchased:** GYG, restaurants, hotels, and tickets on the page are **picks to buy**, not things already paid for. Only write “already booked” if Alex said it is paid. Use “book ahead” / “to book.”
- **Budget:** hero chip, category table, donut `amt`s, and tour bars must be recalculated in that same edit. No leftover $407 on a dropped flight.
- **Photos:** a day’s pictures are that day’s places. No leftover ranch shots on a Palermo day.

## Coverage audit (do this every pass)

For each city, list: vibe neighborhood, what we booked, and the **“people say you can’t miss this”** icons within reach.

If an icon is sitting next to the hotel (or a short Uber) and the day is a generic walking tour, a lecture, or something Alex already said he doesn’t care about, **swap**. Paying for the trip and missing Casa Rosada / Colón / Christ because we booked a 2h history stroll is a fail.

Official tickets + Uber for icons. Guides only to *do* something or for hassle (border, Caminito touts).

## Ratings

- **Food:** TripAdvisor only. **4.7+ stars and 200+ reviews.**
- **Experiences:** GetYourGuide only. **4.7+ stars and 150+ reviews.** Official venue tickets (trains, parks, cable cars) beat a GYG listing that misses the bar.
- Restaurants: TripAdvisor. Hotels: Expedia. Experiences: GYG. Do-it-yourself (Uber + official tickets) when a guide is not earning its keep.

## Fancy plus walk-in (every city)

Keep the reservation dinners. Also put **walk-in / street / kiosk** food on the page so it is not all Fairmont-and-Fogón.

- Label each named Eat line **Reserve** or **Walk-in**.
- Each **Book** line needs a **clock time** (or a pickup window from the listing), not just “morning.” Confirm the live GYG slot on the date before paying.
- Each city gets at least one **must-try delicacy** (açaí, coconut water, cachaça, chipa, helado, alfajores). Put it on a slack day, not on the back of a stacked icon day.
- Named shops still need TripAdvisor **4.7 / 200**. If the famous juice bar or heladería fails the bar, name the **food** and the neighborhood (beach kiosk, Plaza Serrano) and do not invent a listing.
- Street counters and beach kiosks do not need a reservation note. Sit-down temples do.

## Links (do not mix)

Every named pick on the page gets the matching shop, not the hotel’s own site, not Viator, not Booking.com.

- **Hotels →** `expedia.com` property page (or Expedia search for that hotel + our dates if the `.h` ID is missing).
- **Experiences →** `getyourguide.com` activity page. Confirm 4.7 / 150 on that URL before you paste it.
- **Restaurants →** `tripadvisor.com` review page. Confirm 4.7 / 200.
- **Flights →** Capital One Teralink for that exact origin, destination, date, pax.
- **Official tickets** (Reichstag dome, park trains, ICE on bahn.com) beat a weak GYG. Those venue URLs are allowed.
- Hands-on cooking counts as an experience. Tourist dinner-theater does not get a pass on the rating bar.

## What the days feel like

Young, active, not a lecture. Default unless Alex overrules.

- **No slow museum walking tours.** No 3-hour “stand and listen to history” loops. You can look that up. Guides are for *doing* or for logistics (border, neighborhood where you’d get lost or hassled), not for a docent at a painting.
- **Food tour once per unique country**, not once per city. Book it on a **full day** in a city we actually sleep in (wake up and sleep there), not the arrive/leave day. ~2–4h neighborhood walk with tastings. If no GYG listing at our stops clears **4.7 / 150**, skip it. Do not skip an icon or add a weak Lapa/Centro night walk just to check the box.
- **Icons, not homework:** Coliseum / Christ / Berlin Wall / Brandenburg Gate energy. See it, photo, leave. Official tickets + Uber. Do not wrap the icon in a talking city tour.
- **Vibe neighborhoods** like Trastevere: where we sleep, eat, and wander. Name them. Sunset/sunrise spots live here or a short walk away.
- **Every full day gets one booked experience** (usually GYG). Arrival and departure days are exempt. Keep it fun: food, active, scenic, cultural-without-the-classroom.
  - Under ~3h: in-neighborhood (food walk, short activity, sunset-adjacent).
  - Over ~3h: fine when it *leaves* the neighborhood (half/full day: falls, jeep, hang glide).
- Active > sitting. Hike, cook, ride, fly, eat, swim. Cemetery/museum story-walks are optional extras, not the day’s main GYG.

## Pace

- Always pad **travel time** (airport buffers, border, bus to the actual station, Uber with bags).
- Sit down for **food and rest** at normal hours. No stacking a hike, a ticketed icon, and a sunset that needs a second neighborhood in the same day.
- One “big” thing per day. Easy day after a red-eye, a 4h+ transfer, or a physical tour.
- Leave extra early when the next move is international, a ULCC, or a dawn domestic.

## Race / event days

If the trip exists because of a race (HYROX, marathon, etc.), that day and the day before are **not tourist days**.

- Day before: light walk, sit-down food, early night. No GYG, no food tour, no “see the whole city.”
- Race day: hotel, bags, get to the venue, race. Optional 20–40 min neighborhood stroll only if legs and timing allow. After the race, food and bed. Not a second city.
- Morning-after: only what fits before the booked train, with bags already at the station hotel. Schloss / one plaza is enough. Do not book a tour that morning.

## Neighborhoods (relatively safe)

Sleep and night walks in neighborhoods that are **relatively safe for tourists**. Note it on each stop. Prefer that over a cheaper room in a rough block.

- **Safe to walk (hotel + evenings):** Ipanema / Leblon, Paraty Centro, Palermo Soho / Recoleta, Berlin Mitte, quiet residential Munich. Foz centro is ordinary-city fine by day and early evening, not a late bar crawl strip.
- **Day-only, Uber in and out:** Selarón / Lapa, La Boca beyond the Caminito strip, Centro BA after dark, Copacabana beach with phones away. Guide is for touts and getting lost, not for a lecture.
- **Station hotels** (Frankfurt Hbf) are a bags exception: sleep there because the train is at 10:20a. Do not wander the station block at night. Taxi or one stop to the old town, then back.
- Do not put the hotel in a favela, empty industrial strip, or a nightlife block we would not walk at 10p. If an icon sits in a mixed area, visit it, then go home to the safe neighborhood.

## Luggage and hotels

- **Carry-on only.** Leave space for souvenirs. Plan **laundry** (hotel desk is fine) so a third week of clothes is not required.
- If the next move is a **train, bus, or airport with bags**, the hotel must sit next to that station, or the plan must be a **direct taxi/Uber with bags**, never a walk across town dragging luggage before or after the stop.
- Never assume a room is ready before ~2–3p. Drop bags at reception and go.

## Flights

- Book **connections as one itinerary** (one confirmation) when two airlines touch. Split one-ways on a layover are unprotected.
- Shop Capital One Travel (Teralink) when that is how we buy. Prices on the page must match the shopped string.
- **When to buy** belongs on Planning notes (month / weeks out), separate from the clock slot. Buy **all air on one shopping day**. Do not lock the long-haul and leave the regional or ULCC hop for months later. Hotels that same week on a free-cancel rate. Tours and dinners closer in.

## Light

- For every city, look up **sunrise and sunset times for the travel dates**.
- Put the **iconic sunrise or sunset** on a day that already has slack, not on the back of a full ticketed morning.

## Copy on trip pages

No em dashes. Use a period, a comma, or a middle dot `·`.
- Name the **neighborhood** (where we sleep, walk, eat), not just the attraction.
- Do not mention another trip by name on this page. Brandenburg / Florence / HYROX / UK stay off the Brazil–Argentina HTML.
