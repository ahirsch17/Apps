    Trip style (default)

    Active and hungry, not a classroom. Icons with official tickets (Christ, Sugarloaf, falls). Vibe neighborhoods we actually sleep in (Ipanema/Arpoador, Paraty Centro, Palermo Soho). One food tour per country if the listing exists. No slow museum loops.

    • Food tours: once per country, not per city. Argentina: Sherpa Palermo, Apr 21. Brazil: skip. No qualifying GYG food walk at Rio, Paraty, or Foz. Do not hold Apr 11 for one.
    • Icons vs walks: Apr 20 is now Casa Rosada + Colón + Obelisco (the stuff next door we were skipping for a ranch). Recoleta is a self-walk, not a talking tour. La Boca stay is Caminito with a short guide because of touts, not a museum loop. Stay with the group, then Uber home.
    • Neighborhoods: we sleep in relatively safe tourist areas (Ipanema, Paraty Centro, Foz centro, Palermo Soho). Mixed icons are day-only.
    • Pace: overall fine. Two tight days only: Apr 10 Christ + Selarón + Sugarloaf + Marine, and Apr 15 bus + surf + 5:15a. Do not add more to those. Easy days (Apr 9, 11 after the hike, 18, 19) are where walk-in food lives.

    Walk-in food and delicacies

    Keep Marine, Peró, Prosa, Confins, Fogón. Those are Reserve. Also eat stuff that does not need a table.

    • Rio: coconut water and açaí from orla kiosks. Named juice bars (Bibi, etc.) fail 4.7. Sit-down walk-in: Clássico Beach Club Ipanema, 4.9 / 870+.
    • Paraty: cachaça on the jeep. Walk-in burgers at Quintal da Vó, 4.8 / 720. Manuê café is 4.6, so it stays off the page.
    • Foz: chipa from a bakery if you see it. Walk-in Baru for pastel / coxinha / tres leches, 4.9 / 3,000+.
    • BA: dulce de leche helado around Plaza Serrano (Cadore and friends sit at 4.6). Choripán / empanada from a counter, not a named stall under the bar. Alfajores on the Apr 22 class. Food tour Apr 21 is the street-food day.

    Neighborhoods (relatively safe)

    We sleep where we would walk at 10p. Mixed spots are visits, not the hotel.

    • Rio: Samba is Ipanema / Arpoador. That is the right base. Copacabana mosaic is a daytime walk home to Ipanema. Marine is a taxi to a hotel restaurant, not a Lapa night. Selarón is daylight, Uber both ways.
    • Paraty: Centro Histórico is small and lit. Fine after dinner on the cobbles.
    • Foz: Hotel Centro. Taxi after dinner. Do not invent a long walk.
    • BA: Miravida is Palermo Soho. Recoleta is the same kind of neighborhood. Plaza de Mayo / Colón in daylight. Caminito with the GYG guide, then leave. Do not wander the rest of La Boca.

    Paraty bus (not an airport change)

    Rio in and out is still GIG. Paraty is a bus, both ways, then we fly GIG→IGU. The only flight that leaves a different airport than we landed is IGU in (Brazil) → IGR out (Argentina) because the falls sit on the border.

    Yes, book the bus in advance for the morning seats (Tue Apr 13 out, Thu Apr 15 back). Not months ahead · about 1–2 weeks out is enough unless a holiday week sells out.

    • Where: costaverdetransportes.com.br (official) or ClickBus / Busbud if the official site is cranky in English. Origin Rio de Janeiro (Novo Rio) → dest Paraty. Round-trip in one cart is slightly cheaper.
    • Board: Guaranteed boarding is only at Rodoviária Novo Rio, not at Ipanema. Uber from Samba with bags ~30–45 min. Be there ~30 min before departure. Paraty terminal → short taxi to Pousada do Ouro (don’t drag bags over cobbles).
    • Zona Sul van stops (Ipanema/Copacabana) exist on some schedules but are not guaranteed boarding · skip them with luggage.

    Cash vs card

    You do not need a wad of local cash. Cards do most of the trip. Bring a little physical money for kiosks and tips.

    • Brazil: Visa/Mastercard in Ipanema, Paraty, Foz restaurants and hotels. Coconut / açaí kiosks and some Ubers prefer reais in cash or PIX (you will not have PIX). Get ~R$200–400 at a bank ATM on arrival (GIG or Ipanema), not a huge airport exchange. Capital One / no-FX card.
    • Argentina: Pay restaurants and hotels on a foreign Visa/Mastercard in pesos, never USD on the terminal (MEP/tourist rate). Still bring ~$200–300 crisp newer $100 USD bills for the two of you and swap a bit at a proper house in Palermo for kiosks, tips, the odd taxi. Skip airport exchange and ATMs if you can.
    • Do not carry thousands of USD. Do not flash cash on the orla or in Centro.

    Plugs (not a giant adapter block)

    US Type A/B wall plugs will not fit. Phones and laptop bricks are dual-voltage. You need the shape, not a voltage converter, and not a surge “block.”

    • One compact universal adapter with USB-C PD (or two cheap ones: Brazil Type N + Argentina Type I, the Australian three-flat-pin). Type C Euro-round often works as a bonus in hotel USB walls.
    • Brazil: Type N (three round pins). Rio is often 127V. Foz is often 220V. USB chargers do not care. A US hair dryer might.
    • Argentina: Type I, 220V.
    • Skip the bulky cube with 6 outlets. One adapter + a small USB-C brick / Anker-style bank is enough for two phones.
